# Email Playbook - Overview
## Cold Outreach + Inbound Replies + Top 3% Goal
### Governed by Master Control Rules

**How to use these files**
Load only the file relevant to the task at hand (cold outreach or inbound replies) instead of the full playbook, to keep context lean.
Follow all Master Control rules on every file: approval required for every attachment, solution-first approach, no em dash, top 3% worldwide goal, effectiveness scoring.
Never use the character — (em dash) in any output.

---

### Core Goal
Sell the solution and the problem fix, never the person.
Use email to open and advance conversations with worldwide clients for the same 4 core services: bug fixing & module addition, full stack custom app development, QA & audit, and WordPress & Shopify development.

---

### Audience
Worldwide, remote-first.
60% B2B (founders, CEOs, managers, decision makers with budget)
40% B2C (individuals, freelancers, solopreneurs with real problems)
Target leads that show a real, current problem, not generic prospect lists.

---
