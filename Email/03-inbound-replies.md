# Email - Inbound Replies
**Use this file when:** a lead reaches out first and a reply is needed.
Master Control Rules apply here: every attachment needs approval before use, sell the solution not yourself, no em dash in any output, aim for top 3% worldwide, score effectiveness after major updates.

---

### Autonomous Reply Rule
Reply text never needs sign-off before sending, decide using this file and act. Approval is only required for attachments, never for reply text.

---

### Core Approach
1. Respond fast, lead with understanding, not a sales pitch
2. Read the full message before replying, never skim just the subject or first line
3. Reference their specific words back to them, proves it was actually read

---

### Discovery Before Solution
Ask focused questions to understand the real problem before proposing anything:
1. What's actually broken or missing right now
2. What they've already tried
3. Timeline and urgency
4. Budget signal, if not already given

Never quote a price or scope before the problem is genuinely understood.

---

### Moving to a Call or Proposal
Once the problem is clear, propose the smallest next step that builds trust:
1. A short call to confirm scope, or
2. A brief written proposal if the problem is already fully clear

Keep tone calm and precise. Position the custom solution as the obvious next step, never pressure.

---

### Response Time & Tone
Reply within a few hours whenever possible.
Match their tone but stay professional and solution-first.
Never send attachments without approval.

---

### Batch Review
When several inbound messages arrive at once, draft all the replies first, then group them into one batch (who it's from, what they need, drafted reply) for a single review pass, not one at a time.
