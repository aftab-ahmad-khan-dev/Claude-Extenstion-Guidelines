# Shared - Human Voice Rules
**Use this file when:** the final pass on any draft before it sends or posts, on any platform.
Governed by Master Control Rules. No em dash in any output.
Applies to every post, comment, DM, email, and proposal in this repo. Content that reads as AI-generated kills trust and kills reach, this file is the pass every draft runs through before it goes out.

---

### Why This Matters
Buyers and platforms both penalize generic, AI-sounding writing. Buyers stop trusting it, algorithms show it to fewer people, and it undercuts the whole point of selling through demonstrated competence: proving it through the writing itself, not by claiming it.

---

### AI-Tell Red Flags (remove every one of these)
- Throat-clearing openers: "In today's fast-paced world," "In the ever-evolving landscape of," "As a developer, I understand..."
- Paragraph-starting connectors: "Moreover," "Furthermore," "Additionally," "In conclusion"
- Perfectly balanced triads: "fast, reliable, and affordable," "not only... but also..."
- Generic hype with no specifics: "game-changing," "unlock your potential," "seamless experience," "cutting-edge"
- Uniform sentence length and rhythm, every sentence the same length with no variation
- Over-explaining things the reader already understands
- Heavy hedging: "it's important to note that," "one could argue"
- A closing recap that just restates what the message already said
- Decorative emoji that carry no meaning

---

### What Real Human Writing Does
1. Starts mid-thought sometimes, like the conversation is already happening
2. Uses contractions always (I'm, don't, that's, it's)
3. Varies sentence length hard. One short one. Then a longer one that actually carries the detail and the reasoning behind it.
4. Leads with one concrete, specific detail (a real number, a real file, a real screenshot) instead of a general claim
5. Lets a little real opinion or tone show through ("honestly," "not gonna lie," "here's the thing")
6. Asks one real, specific question instead of a generic "let me know if you have questions"
7. Can end on a fragment or an informal beat, a small imperfection reads as real, not sloppy

---

### The Human Pass: Step-by-Step
Run this on every draft before sending or posting:
1. Read it out loud. If a phrase would sound strange said to a friend, rewrite it
2. Cut every sentence that could be deleted without losing meaning
3. Replace any generic adjective ("amazing," "powerful," "robust") with a specific detail or number
4. Strike every phrase from the Red Flags list above
5. Make sure at least one sentence in the piece is short and blunt
6. Read the first line alone. Would it stop a scroll, earn a reply, or get ignored as spam
