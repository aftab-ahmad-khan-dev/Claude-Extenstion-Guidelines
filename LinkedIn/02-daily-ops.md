# LinkedIn Daily Operations

### Session Priority Order

1. **Inbox First**
   - Reply to existing conversations and comments on your posts
   - Respond to new connection acceptances with a discovery question

2. **High-Value Engagement**
   - Comment only when you can add genuine technical insight
   - Target: 8–12 high-quality comments maximum

3. **Connection Requests**
   - Send only to people who show clear relevance
   - Target: 10–18 requests per day maximum
   - Prefer warm signals (people who engaged with your content or shared connections)

4. **Content Creation**
   - Prepare 1 strong post (aim for 3–5 per week)
   - Create Canva visual when it adds clear value

5. **End of Session**
   - Present one clean batch for approval
   - Include scores and reasoning
