# Meta - Scheduling System
**Use this file when:** scheduling posts or deciding posting frequency.
Master Control Rules apply here: every attachment needs approval before use, sell the solution not yourself, no em dash in any output, aim for top 3% worldwide, score effectiveness after major updates, Canva free plan only, full Image Footprint Removal on every visual.

---

### Autonomous Scheduling Rule
Once a post's copy and visuals are approved, scheduling it never needs a separate sign-off, queue it and move on. Approval applies to the attachment itself, not the act of scheduling.

---

### Scheduling: Step-by-Step (Instagram, Facebook)
1. Confirm the post's visual has already been approved, per Master Control Rules
2. Schedule it in Meta Business Suite at a time supported by recent platform insights, not a fixed default slot
3. Queue a batch of the week's approved posts together in one session rather than scheduling one at a time as each is finished
4. Recommended frequency: 4-7 posts per week across Instagram and Facebook, adjusted by what insights show is actually working

---

### Threads: Step-by-Step
1. Threads currently has no Meta Business Suite scheduling, post directly in the Threads app
2. Post or reply most days, Threads rewards frequent, in-the-moment participation more than a fixed weekly count
3. Treat an active reply thread as equally important as a new post, participation there compounds visibility

---

### Batch Review
When multiple posts are ready to schedule at once, present the whole week's queue together (post, platform, planned time) for one review, not post by post.
