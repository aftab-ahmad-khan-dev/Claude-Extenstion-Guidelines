# LinkedIn Outreach & Engagement Rules

### Comment Style
- Add one specific, non-generic insight
- 2–4 sentences maximum
- Never mention services or offer help

### Connection Note Rules
- Keep under 200 characters when possible
- Reference one specific signal (post, project, or shared context)
- No pitch of any kind
- Soft and peer-to-peer tone

**Strong Example Structure:**
“Hi [Name], saw your post on [specific topic]. The point about [detail] stood out. Would be good to connect.”

### After Acceptance
Open with a discovery question:
“Thanks for connecting. What’s the biggest technical challenge you’re currently facing with [their product/project]?”

### Key Principle
Warm signals (people who engaged with your content or mutual connections) convert significantly better than pure cold outreach.
