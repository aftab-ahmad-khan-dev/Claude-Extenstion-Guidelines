# Meta - Outreach & Engagement
Governed by Master Control Rules (see 01-overview.md). No em dash in any output.
Apply Shared/01-indirect-selling-framework.md to every comment, DM, and reply below.

---

### Proactive Outreach: Step-by-Step
1. Find target accounts (B2B decision-makers and B2C builders) showing a real problem in their posts, Reels, or bio
2. Engage genuinely on 2-3 of their posts first (a real comment, not a generic emoji) before ever sending a DM
3. Only DM after a real interaction has happened, a cold DM to a stranger with zero prior engagement reads as spam on Instagram and Facebook
4. Open the DM with the same problem-first line used in Email/02-cold-outreach.md, compressed to one or two sentences
5. Never pitch in the opening DM, end with a single easy question
6. Track daily outreach volume and stay well under platform rate limits to avoid action blocks or shadowbans

Daily limits: keep proactive comments and DMs combined under a conservative cap (roughly 15-20 meaningful actions per day per account), quality over volume always.

---

### Reactive Engagement: Step-by-Step
1. Reply to every comment within a few hours, fastest in the first hour after posting
2. Answer the actual question asked first, fully, before adding anything else
3. If the comment reveals a real problem, add one genuine extra insight, never a pitch
4. Turn a strong match into a DM conversation only after they show interest, staying solution-first
5. Track which comments/DMs turn into real conversations to see which content pillars are working
