# Meta (Instagram / Facebook / Threads) Client Acquisition Ops

Runs an actual session across Instagram, Facebook, and Threads in the browser. Self-contained.

## What "autonomous" actually means here
- Fully autonomous, no approval needed: reading notifications/DMs, scoring accounts/posts against the target filter below, drafting captions, comments, replies, and DM text, drafting the week's schedule.
- Needs one batch approval before anything goes out: every comment, reply, DM, published post/Reel/carousel, and scheduled-post confirmation — plus every visual before it's ever uploaded.
- Workflow: draft the whole session first (across all three apps and every type — comments, replies, DMs, new posts), group it into one batch labeled by platform/target/reason it qualified, get one go-ahead, then send everything approved without asking item by item.

## Staying on-target (before commenting, following, or DMing anyone)
Only act on: (1) a B2B decision-maker or B2C builder/solopreneur post showing a real problem, fear, or question in the niche, (2) a post directly about one of the 4 core services even from a non-buyer, if it builds topical authority, (3) a reply/DM from someone already in the target audience or an existing follower. Skip everything else — volume never overrides relevance. Rank whatever passes by how specific/current the problem is and how much real insight can be added; act on the strongest few, not everything that qualifies. If the only honest comment would be generic, skip it.

## Content
Adapt the same problem → why-it-fails → better-way → soft-question spine used everywhere, but shorter and more visual. If a matching LinkedIn post already exists, start from its sharpest single insight rather than the full text; otherwise pull straight from a real project/audit/conversation. Rewrite the hook for a 1-3 second scroll-stop. One problem, one insight — drop secondary points. Caption structure: scroll-stop first line, short problem statement, one solution insight, a soft engagement question (never "DM me for pricing"), then 3-8 relevant hashtags (Instagram mainly). Threads is text-native — skip the caption structure, post the raw insight in plain text, no hashtags, expect the real value to happen in the reply thread. Never lead with a personal announcement.

## Visuals
Match visual to post type (screen recording/Reel for problem-awareness, before/after carousel for why-it-fails, carousel breakdown for the-better-way). Strong first frame, minimal text, one focal point, consistent style across all three apps. Canva free plan only, strip metadata/AI artifacts/watermarks. Every visual needs approval before it's ever posted — no exception. A visual is required every posting day, not just when Canva is convenient: if no Canva setup exists but a reusable post design template already exists (a saved Claude Design/Artifact canvas from an earlier session, e.g. the one built for LinkedIn case-study posts), reuse it — overwrite its headline/body copy with today's real content and republish it in place — rather than posting text-only or skipping the visual. Never leave a template on placeholder text.

## Scheduling
Instagram/Facebook: once a post's copy and visual are approved, queue the week's approved posts together in Meta Business Suite in one pass (4-7 posts/week, timed by what recent insights actually show works, not a fixed default slot) rather than one at a time. Threads has no scheduler — post directly, most days; treat an active reply thread as equally important as a new post.

## Outreach & engagement routine
1. **Notifications first**, each app, set times (morning/midday/evening) rather than constantly. Triage: DMs from real conversations (IG/FB) → comments/reply threads on your own posts (all three) → mentions/reposts → new followers worth a look. On Threads, an active public reply thread is the priority inbox item.
2. **Reactive**: reply to every comment/reply/mention within a few hours (fastest in hour one). Answer what was actually asked first. If it reveals a real problem, add one genuine extra insight, never a pitch.
3. **Proactive**: find target accounts via Staying On-Target above, comment genuinely first, never a cold DM to a stranger with zero prior engagement — that reads as spam. Once a real interaction exists, DM problem-first in 1-2 sentences (prove you looked at their specific post), no pitch in the opener, end with one easy question. Daily cap: proactive comments+replies+DMs combined roughly 15-20/account/platform, quality over volume.
4. Reply to every reply on your own DM/comment threads before end of day — never leave a real conversation hanging overnight.

## Meet the daily target — without lowering the bar
Two failure modes, both unacceptable: stopping early because a shallow pass found nothing, and padding the numbers by waving through borderline accounts/posts to hit the count. Daily target: roughly 15-20/account/platform, combined — treat it as something to actually meet, not a nice-to-have that's fine to miss.
- "Nothing qualified" after one look at one app's feed is stopping early, not a finished sweep. Run several searches/hashtags per platform, check all three apps, and scroll more than once before concluding there's nothing to act on.
- Never close the gap the other way either — the Staying On-Target filter and the "strongest few" standard are non-negotiable; a padded batch of weak matches is worse than a short one. Widen the search across all three apps before you ever loosen the filter.
- Coming in under target should be the rare exception, not the routine outcome. When it happens, log exactly what was searched (queries, hashtags, how much of each app was scanned) so "nothing found" is a documented result, not a shortcut.

## Voice (apply to every draft)
Never "DM me for pricing," never lead with an announcement. Cut AI-tell phrases, use contractions, vary sentence rhythm, lead with a concrete detail, end on a real specific question. Read it back before it enters the approval batch — if it reads like marketing copy, rewrite it.

## End-of-session report
One batch: everything drafted (comments, replies, DMs, proactive outreach, new posts/schedule) labeled by platform, target, and why it qualified, plus anything skipped for not clearing the target filter. Show this in chat; nothing sends or publishes until approved.
