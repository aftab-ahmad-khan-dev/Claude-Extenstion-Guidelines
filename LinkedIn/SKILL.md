Draw topics from Aftab's full expertise list above, rotating deliberately
Two visual formats are available. Default to the single-image poster. Use a carousel only when the topic genuinely needs step-by-step depth:
Single-image poster (preferred, "diamond" style): one high-polish 1080x1350 image. Structure: a short two-line hook (the second line opens a curiosity gap), one dominant bold headline with a color accent on the key phrase, a short subtitle, a row of 3 to 5 uniform icon cards (each a short label plus one line of detail), and a bottom callout bar with a single punchy takeaway. Tight, high-contrast color system. Newsjack a current or culturally relevant topic when it fits naturally, so the post travels.
Carousel (when depth helps): 1080x1350, 8 to 12 slides, clean and readable.
Build the poster or carousel as self-contained HTML rendered to PNG at exactly 1080x1350 (headless Chromium works well), or in Canva. Check 08-canva-template.md for the current Canva template.
Present both text and visual together for approval
No em dashes anywhere in the copy, write the way a person actually talks
