# Shared - Repurposing, Proof & Metrics
**Use this file when:** turning one real insight into coverage across every platform, or reviewing what's actually working.
Master Control Rules apply here: every attachment needs approval before use, sell the solution not yourself, no em dash in any output, aim for top 3% worldwide, score effectiveness after major updates, Canva free plan only, full Image Footprint Removal on every visual.
Covers what the original single-file playbooks were missing: turning one idea into full coverage, capturing proof, and tracking what actually predicts revenue.

---

### Content Repurposing Engine
1. Start every content idea from a real problem actually solved or observed (a project, an audit, a buyer request, an inbound email)
2. Write the long-form version first (LinkedIn post or newsletter)
3. Cut it into a short-form video script (Meta Reels / LinkedIn native video) using the same problem, why it fails, better way structure
4. Cut it into a carousel/document post using the same structure, one point per slide
5. Cut the single sharpest insight into a cold-outreach opening line or subject line
6. Log the idea and every format it became in the content bank so nothing is wasted after one post

---

### Content Bank (swipe file)
Keep a running list of:
- Real problems observed in projects, buyer requests, and inbound emails (the raw material)
- Which formats/pillars each one was turned into
- Which ones over-performed, so the pattern can be reused with a new problem

---

### Proof & Social Proof System
Every completed project is a potential proof asset once approved by the client.
1. Ask for a short testimonial or permission to share results (numbers, before/after, screenshots) right after delivery, while satisfaction is highest
2. Store anonymized versions (no client name or brand unless explicitly permitted) for reuse across LinkedIn, Meta, and gig galleries
3. Never fabricate or exaggerate a result

---

### Metrics That Actually Matter
Track weekly, in this order of priority, above raw likes:
1. Saves and shares (signals real value, drives algorithmic reach)
2. Profile visits and follows from a specific post
3. Comments and DMs that turn into a real conversation
4. Connection acceptance rate and reply rate on outreach
5. Calls booked / proposals sent that trace back to content

Vanity likes are a lagging, weak signal, do not optimize for them directly.
