# Shared - Content Trends: Current & Near-Future
Governed by Master Control Rules. No em dash in any output.
Scan this before planning a content batch. Treat it as a living document, not a fixed list, update it as formats shift.

---

### Trend Radar Process
Run this monthly, before writing the next content batch:
1. Check what is currently over-performing in your niche on LinkedIn and Meta, save 5-10 real examples
2. Note the format, not just the topic (carousel vs text vs video vs poll)
3. Note which real problem or fear each one is built around
4. Adapt the pattern to your own real expertise, never copy the post itself
5. Retire any format that underperforms 2-3 times in a row

---

### Current Format Trends
- Short vertical video (Reels, native LinkedIn video) still wins on reach, best for problem-teardown and screen-recording content
- Native document/carousel posts outperform single static images for saves on LinkedIn
- "Build in public" or process content (the actual debugging, audit, or build, not just the result) outperforms polished announcement posts
- POV / screen-recording breakdowns ("here's what I found auditing a real app") outperform generic advice posts
- Text-only high-insight posts still work on LinkedIn when the first 1-2 lines earn the expand click
- Polls and open questions used sparingly to spark comments, never as filler

---

### Near-Future Direction (prepare for this now)
- **Answer-engine visibility**: being the source cited or summarized by AI search assistants is becoming as important as ranking in search engines. Write content that is quotable, structured, and factually precise, so it is easy for AI tools to surface and cite.
- **Authenticity over polish** keeps increasing in weight, both algorithmically and with buyers. Raw screen recordings and real, client-approved before/afters keep outperforming stock-style graphics.
- **Video-first discovery** is expanding past Reels into LinkedIn native video and platform search itself.
- **Micro-niche authority** (owning one specific problem type extremely well, e.g. "vibecoded app audits") increasingly outperforms broad generalist positioning.
- **DM-based nurture** (a public comment moving into a private conversation) is becoming a bigger share of pipeline than public CTAs ever were.

---

### How to Apply
Do not chase every trend. Pick 2-3 formats per platform that genuinely fit the real work (audits, bug fixes, builds) and go deep on those, using the Indirect Selling Framework (01-indirect-selling-framework.md) as the content spine regardless of format.
