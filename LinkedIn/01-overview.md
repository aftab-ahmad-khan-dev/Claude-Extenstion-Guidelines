# LinkedIn Playbook - Overview

**Core Goal**  
Turn LinkedIn into a consistent client acquisition channel by building authority through specific, useful content and high-quality conversations.

**Primary ICP**
- Founders, CEOs, CTOs, and Product Managers of startups and growing companies
- Looking for: AI features, custom software, MVPs, full-stack development, automation
- Strong signals: Building products, technical challenges, hiring developers, launching features
- Priority markets: US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Europe

**Secondary ICP**
- Solopreneurs and indie hackers who have outgrown no-code tools and need professional development

**Core Principles**
- Sell the solution, never yourself
- Specificity beats generic advice
- Quality conversations > high volume
- Always get human approval before any action is sent or published
- Consistency compounds (3–5 strong posts per week is enough)
