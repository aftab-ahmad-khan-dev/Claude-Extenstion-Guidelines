# Meta - Outreach & Engagement (Instagram, Facebook, Threads)
**Use this file when:** finding and engaging target accounts, or handling notifications, comments, replies, and DMs across Instagram, Facebook, and Threads. Draft the whole session's engagement first, then present it as one batch for a single review pass, never item by item.
Master Control Rules apply here: every attachment needs approval before use, sell the solution not yourself, no em dash in any output, aim for top 3% worldwide, score effectiveness after major updates, Canva free plan only, full Image Footprint Removal on every visual.
Sell the solution, never yourself, in every comment, reply, and DM: add a real, specific insight or ask a real question, never mention services, never say "I can help with this."

---

### Autonomous Engagement Rule
Engagement runs on judgment, not approval. Comments, replies, and DMs never need sign-off before sending, decide using this file, then act. Approval is only required for attachments and visuals (Master Control Rules), never for engagement text.

---

### Staying On-Target
Not every post is worth engaging with, on any of the three apps. Before commenting, following, or DMing, confirm it's actually one of:
1. A post from the target audience (B2B decision-makers or B2C builders/solopreneurs) showing a real problem, a fear, or a question in the niche
2. A post directly about one of the 4 core services (bug fixing, full stack development, QA & audit, WordPress & Shopify), even from a peer or non-buyer, if it builds topical authority
3. A reply or DM from someone already in the target audience or an existing follower

Skip everything outside these three. Volume never overrides relevance.

---

### Finding the Best Matches, Not Every Match
Passing the Staying On-Target filter makes a post or account eligible, it doesn't make it worth acting on. Before engaging or building a batch for review:
1. Scan broadly across Instagram, Facebook, and Threads, but only shortlist posts/accounts that clearly pass Staying On-Target
2. Rank the shortlist by strength of match: how specific and current the problem is, how clearly the poster fits the B2B/B2C target profile, how much real insight can genuinely be added
3. Act on (or present, if a batch is being reviewed) only the strongest few matches per platform, not everything that merely qualifies
4. Drop anything where the only honest comment would be generic, if there's nothing specific and real to add, skip it rather than force one

---

### Proactive Outreach: Step-by-Step
1. Find target accounts and posts using Staying On-Target and Finding the Best Matches above, act on the strongest few, not everything that qualifies, on Instagram, Facebook, or Threads
2. Engage genuinely first (a real comment or reply, not a generic emoji) before ever sending a DM
3. Only DM after a real interaction has happened, a cold DM to a stranger with zero prior engagement reads as spam
4. Open the DM problem-first, in one or two sentences: prove you looked at their specific post or situation, then state the problem back to them, no greeting needed, no formal letter tone
5. Never pitch in the opening message, end with a single easy question
6. Track daily outreach volume and stay well under platform rate limits to avoid action blocks or shadowbans

Daily limits: keep proactive comments, replies, and DMs combined under a conservative cap (roughly 15-20 meaningful actions per day per account, per platform), quality over volume always.

---

### Reactive Engagement: Step-by-Step
1. Reply to every comment, reply, or mention within a few hours, fastest in the first hour after posting
2. Answer the actual question asked first, fully, before adding anything else
3. If the comment reveals a real problem, add one genuine extra insight, never a pitch
4. Turn a strong match into a DM conversation only after they show interest, staying solution-first
5. Track which comments, replies, and DMs turn into real conversations to see which content pillars are working

Threads note: Threads runs on public reply threads rather than a DM inbox, treat a good reply thread there the same as a DM conversation elsewhere, and if it warrants a private conversation, move it to Instagram once it's warm.

---

### Notifications & Inbox: Daily Routine
1. Check the Activity/notifications tab on each app (Instagram, Facebook, Threads) at set times, morning, midday, evening, rather than constantly
2. Triage in this order: DMs from real conversations first (Instagram, Facebook), then comments and reply threads on your own posts across all three apps, then mentions and reposts, then new followers worth a look
3. Reply to DMs the same way as any inbound inquiry: understanding before solution, one discovery question (what's actually broken, what's already been tried, timeline) before proposing anything or quoting a price
4. On Threads, treat an active public reply thread as the priority inbox item, that is where most of the real back-and-forth happens
5. Never leave a real conversation unanswered past the end of the day, on any of the three apps

---

### Daily Batch Review
Everything above is "engagement": comments, replies, DM replies, notification-triggered replies, and proactive comments/DMs to new accounts. The target audience is already defined above, don't re-check fit with the user, only apply Staying On-Target and Finding the Best Matches.
1. Draft every item for the session first, across every app and every type, before presenting anything
2. Group the draft into one batch: comments/replies, DM replies, proactive outreach messages, each item labeled with the platform, who it's to, and why it passed the filter
3. Present the whole batch once for a single review pass, never ask about one item at a time
4. After the batch is approved, send everything in it, no further per-item confirmation needed

---

### Cross-Platform Consistency
Keep the same weekly problem/insight recognizable across every platform, reformatted per each one's own rules, so the brand feels consistent even to someone who only sees one of them.
