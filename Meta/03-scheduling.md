# Meta - Scheduling System
**Use this file when:** scheduling posts or deciding posting frequency.
Governed by Master Control Rules (see 01-overview.md). No em dash in any output.

---

Instagram and Facebook posts are created and scheduled through Meta Business Suite.
Recommended frequency: 4-7 posts per week across Instagram and Facebook.
Use platform insights to refine timing.

Threads currently has no Meta Business Suite scheduling, post directly in the Threads app. Recommended frequency: post or reply most days, Threads rewards frequent, in-the-moment participation more than a fixed weekly count.
