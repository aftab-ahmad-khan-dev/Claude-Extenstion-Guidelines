# LinkedIn Profile Optimization

**Use this file when:** reviewing or updating the LinkedIn profile.

### Goal
Make the profile convert visitors into conversations.  
The profile should clearly communicate who you help and what problems you solve — without sounding salesy.

### Key Sections to Optimize

**1. Profile Photo**
- Clear, professional headshot
- Good lighting, neutral or clean background
- Face clearly visible

**2. Banner Image**
- Simple and clean
- Reinforce who you help or the type of problems you solve
- Avoid clutter

**3. Headline**
- Focus on the outcome or problem you solve
- Avoid generic titles like “Founder” or “Software Developer”
- Strong format example:  
  “I help founders build reliable AI-powered products and MVPs”

**4. About Section**
Structure:
- Start with the main problem your ideal clients face
- Briefly explain your approach or perspective
- Mention the type of work you do (AI features, custom software, MVPs, full-stack)
- End with a soft, low-pressure invitation to connect or message

**5. Featured Section**
- Add your best posts, case studies, or useful frameworks
- Keep it updated with high-performing content

**6. Experience**
- Focus on outcomes and problems solved rather than just responsibilities
- Use clear, specific language

### Profile Rules
- Keep everything natural and human
- Never over-optimize with keyword stuffing
- Make sure every claim is true
- Review the profile every 4–6 weeks or when performance drops
