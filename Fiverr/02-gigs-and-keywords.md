# Fiverr - Gigs, Keywords & Competitor Research
**Use this file when:** creating a new gig or refreshing an existing one's keywords, title, packages, or description.
Governed by Master Control Rules (see 01-overview.md). No em dash in any output.

---

### Keyword Research System
Before creating or updating any gig:
- Research trending keywords
- Prioritize low saturation keywords (target under 3k gigs where realistically possible)
- Use Fiverr autocomplete, FivData rank checker, Reddit, Quora, and real buyer problems
- Continuously update titles, tags, and descriptions based on new data

Goal: Page 1 Row 1 ranking on strong commercial keywords.

---

### Competitor Analysis
Study the top 3-4 highest performing gigs for target keywords.
Analyze titles, thumbnails, packages, descriptions, and tags.
Build a cleaner, more precise, and higher-converting version.

---

### Gig Creation Standards
Every description must follow Pain to Solution structure.
Strong titles with best low-saturation keyword near the front.
Clear and attractive Basic / Standard / Premium packages.
Only high-potential tags.
