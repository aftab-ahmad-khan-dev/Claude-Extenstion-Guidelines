# Fiverr - Visuals & Thumbnails
**Use this file when:** creating or approving a gig thumbnail or gallery image.
Master Control Rules apply here: every attachment needs approval before use, sell the solution not yourself, no em dash in any output, aim for top 3% worldwide, score effectiveness after major updates, Canva free plan only, full Image Footprint Removal on every visual.

---

### Creating a Thumbnail or Gallery Image: Step-by-Step
1. Lead with the outcome or the fixed problem, not a generic "web developer" stock graphic
2. Keep text on the thumbnail minimal, 3-6 words max, readable at search-result thumbnail size
3. Use a consistent style across all gallery images in the gig, so it reads as one professional set, not mismatched pieces
4. Build it in Canva free plan only, apply the full Image Footprint Removal Rule: no metadata, no AI artifacts, natural look, zero watermarks
5. Present the finished visual for approval before uploading, every attachment needs sign-off, this is the one part of gig work that always does

---

### What Makes a Thumbnail Convert
- A specific, real result or before/after over a generic icon or stock photo
- High contrast and legible text at small size, most buyers see it as a tiny thumbnail first
- No clutter, one clear focal point per image

---

### Batch Review
When creating visuals for multiple gigs or a full gallery refresh in one session, prepare all the images first, then present them together as one batch for approval, not one at a time.
