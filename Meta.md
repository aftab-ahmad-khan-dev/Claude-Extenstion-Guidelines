# Claude Meta Playbook
## Instagram + Facebook via Meta Business Suite
### Governed by Master Control Rules

**How to use this file**
Proceed one by one.
Follow all Master Control rules: approval required for every attachment, solution-first approach, no em dash, top 3% worldwide goal, effectiveness scoring, Canva free plan only, and full Image Footprint Removal.
Never use the character — (em dash) in any output.

---

### Core Goal
Sell the solution and the problem fix, never the person.
Build strong visibility, engagement, and authority on Instagram and Facebook that supports the overall top 3% goal and drives quality conversations.

---

### Content Strategy
Take the same core ideas used on LinkedIn and adapt them properly for Meta.
Make content more visual, shorter in hook, and mobile-native while keeping the same problem → solution messaging.

---

### Scheduling System
All posts are created and scheduled through Meta Business Suite.
Recommended frequency: 4–7 posts per week across Instagram and Facebook.
Use platform insights to refine timing.

---

### Caption Structure
1. Strong scroll-stopping first line
2. Short and clear problem statement
3. Calm and precise solution insight
4. Soft engagement question
5. Relevant hashtags (mainly for Instagram)

---

### Platform-Specific Notes
- Instagram: Higher visual focus, Reels and carousels perform well, use 3–8 relevant hashtags
- Facebook: Slightly more conversational tone, fewer hashtags needed

---

### Visual Standards
All images, carousels, and Reel covers require user approval first.
Use Canva free plan only.
Apply full Image Footprint Removal Rule.
Maintain consistent professional style across both platforms.

---

### Engagement Rules
Reply to comments quickly and in the same calm voice.
Turn meaningful conversations into potential client discussions when natural.
Track saves, shares, comments, and profile visits closely.

---

### Cross-Platform Consistency
Keep weekly themes aligned with LinkedIn content so the brand feels consistent across all platforms.

---

### Effectiveness Scoring
After every content batch provide scores for:
- Impressions and reach potential
- Engagement potential (reactions, comments, shares, saves)
- Profile visits potential
- Overall progress versus previous version
- Contribution toward top 3% goal

---

### Final Rules
- Every attachment must be approved before use
- Skip any section that is already performing well
- Maximize impressions, engagement, profile visibility, and supporting conversions
- The solution and problem-fix are always the hero
